Our home planet

Earth

Earth is the only known planet with liquid water on the surface and life as we know it. It is wrapped in a protective atmosphere and surrounded by a system of oceans, weather, and ecosystems.

  • 71% of Earth is covered by water
  • One natural satellite: the Moon
  • Home to millions of species
Atmosphere

Life support

Our atmosphere protects life from harmful radiation and keeps temperatures stable.

Oceans

Blue planet

Earth’s oceans shape climate, weather, and the incredible variety of life.

Earth’s companion

The Moon

Surface

Rocky, dusty, and marked with craters formed by impacts from space.

Orbit

The Moon travels around Earth and helps create tides on our oceans.

Exploration

Humans have explored it, and future missions aim to return with new science.

Human achievement

NASA Missions

1969

Apollo 11

First humans landed on the Moon, changing the future of space exploration forever.

1990

Hubble Space Telescope

Revealed stunning views of galaxies, nebulae, and deep-space phenomena.

2012

Curiosity Rover

Explored Mars and found evidence that the planet once had watery environments.

2020+

Artemis Program

NASA is preparing the next generation of astronauts to return to the Moon.
